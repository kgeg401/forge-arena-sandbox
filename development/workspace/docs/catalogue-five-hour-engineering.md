## Limits and next work

The 289 proposals are not 289 new supported cards. Most were already admitted
under earlier bounded evidence; the research policy contains 303 main-deck names,
25 more than the verified baseline. A trample creature entering the battlefield
does not prove trample damage allocation. Every required keyword, choice,
interaction, original-client presentation and full-match gate remains separate.
Catalogue presence also does not prove retail release status, ownership or format
legality. Unknown source behavior is retained as a review requirement.

Continue with worker order/failure/timeout isolation, cold samples covering the
generated families, explicit coverage aggregation, unsupported human-choice
probes, native choices and complete-match verification. Consider additional
mechanics only after their missing interfaces and test oracles are established.
Do not grant support by adding names to an admission list.
