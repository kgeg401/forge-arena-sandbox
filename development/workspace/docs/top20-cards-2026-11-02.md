# Top 20 Commander lists: bounded compatibility work

This pass uses the 20 most-viewed public Commander decks returned by Moxfield
on 2026-11-02. It retains the requested Arena-existing-only limit. The private
capture stores exact ranking, retrieval time, deck IDs, lists and source hashes.

The lists contain 937 distinct names: 634 match the installed Arena catalogue;
303 are outside this pass. All 634 Arena names were already admitted by the
installed client. No new primary admission was necessary. This does **not**
certify every effect or make the original 20 lists Arena-legal: some commanders
and other list cards are outside the Arena pool.

## Fixes

- Export modal **triggered** abilities from Forge's actual trigger ability,
  rather than only spell/activated modes. Recover the original Arena parent
  and child identities for Dawnbringer Cleric, Hullbreaker Horror, Oltec
  Matterweaver and Plundering Barbarian.
- Compose those four `modalBinding` changes into the complete consumer overlay.
  Fresh reconstruction preserves all 1,939 presentation objects and 1,791
  primary admissions, with no unrelated binding changes or new admissions.
- Offer K'rrik's ordinary black-mana life substitution when it is the legal
  payment, including partial mana/life payments. Preserve the player's choice
  to use mana.
- Commit tentative mana-life payments only after the complete mana payment
  succeeds, matching the original Forge human input. A canceled payment returns
  the spell without consuming tentative life.
- Distinguish an explicitly declined, uncompleted mana payment from a failed
  action, so normal cancellation does not suspend automatic progression.
  Unavailable actions and unsupported callbacks remain errors.
- Preserve the requested deck count in the collection/audit provenance. Older
  captures require a complete matching companion ranking receipt.

## Evidence and limits

Focused original-handler scenarios cover Oracle/Pact, Ritual/Brain Freeze,
two real Breach escapes, Winota's arriving attacker/double strike, Bowmasters
draw triggers, Delney/Tataru's once-per-turn restriction, Oltec's two contextual
modes, Powerbalance matches/mismatches, and K'rrik's life, mana, mixed,
insufficient-life and canceled payment paths. Modal tests independently check
all three Cleric modes, both Barbarian modes and Hullbreaker's spell/permanent
bounce and optional decline. A normal shuffled Commander game separately checks
K'rrik's fourth-land cast and cancellation through the normal turn controller.

Existing payment/effect regressions cover additional shared interactions.
Additional scenarios check Force of Will's real alternate life/exile cost,
Beseech the Mirror's bargain sacrifice and free cast, The One Ring's growing
burden/draw and upkeep life loss across two real turn cycles, and its player
protection excluding the player while leaving their creature targetable.
Mana Vault's three-mana activation, unpaid upkeep, retained tapped state and
draw-step damage also pass. The combined final-source run passes 24 scenarios
and 128 checks in two independent original-handler workers.
Their helper now uses the completed original native handler's state rather than
a potentially older `state.json` publication; the retained Necropotence failure
was that observation race, not a rules-engine change. The corrected test checks
real private exile and the original end-step return.

A separate original Unity client renders the Barbarian's original mode cards,
accepts the original selectable-card callback and creates the real Treasure.
K'rrik's original life prompt, canceled payment and same-match retry also have
rendered evidence. These use seeded private scenarios and original handlers;
they are not full-deck matchup or exhaustive animation-parity evidence.
