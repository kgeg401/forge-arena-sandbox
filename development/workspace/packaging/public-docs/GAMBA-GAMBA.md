# Gamba Gamba

A 100-card blue/red Commander deck led by Yusri, Fortune's Flame. The requested
"bracket 4.8" is an informal high-power target, not an official rating or a tested
competitive result. The list includes paper cards and belongs in the local
Commander/Horde queues rather than official Arena Brawl.

The list uses an extra Island instead of Gemstone Caverns: its optional opening-
hand ability currently reaches an unreliable native choice panel. This avoids
that known opening stall; it does not claim the general choice bug is repaired.

- Attack with Yusri for up to five flips; Krark's Thumb improves the offered
  outcomes. Five wins allow free spells from your hand for the turn.
- Tavern Scoundrel produces Treasures and Chance Encounter accumulates luck.
- Activate Frenetic Efreet repeatedly before allowing the first ability to
  resolve. The queued abilities still flip even if an earlier one removes or
  phases out the Efreet. Ten luck counters win at your next upkeep.
- Okaun, Zndrsplt, clone effects, extra combat/turn spells and spell copying add
  more opportunities. Breach/Petal/Brain Freeze provides a separate combo line.

Rolls and triggers use Forge's original engine. Heads/Tails and Krark's Thumb
choices use the client's existing native choice panel; no substitute forced
coin result or new coin animation is used. Automatic replacement choices may be
enabled in the existing settings for fewer prompts during large stacks.

The new entries remain explicitly experimental. Core flip/draw/damage/Treasure/
luck interactions and a bounded 24-activation stack have focused native-handler
tests; that does not certify every combination of all 100 cards. Original card
illustrations are downloaded to the private cache, with artist credits retained.
