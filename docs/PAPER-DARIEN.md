# FREAK-OFF Darien (Paper Commander)

The sample deck is the original FREAK-OFF Darien list: **99 main cards plus
Darien, King of Kjeldor**. No cards are replaced. It includes 33 Plains,
Myriad Landscape, Nomad Stadium and Sunscorched Desert.

Open Play, choose **Zombie Horde (Commander Deck)** under Brawl, then select
**FREAK-OFF Darien (Paper Commander)**. You start at **40 life**, receive
**six preparation turns**, and face the normal **200-card Zombie/Horror Horde**.
The same deck can also use the local Commander Bot and experimental Commander Pod.
These are sandbox formats; the paper list is not an official Arena-legal deck.

The list has 68 distinct names. Fifty-two needed new client admission; 31 of
those have no printing in the pinned Arena installation. Those paper-only cards
use separate local presentation identities and the existing Forge rules engine.
They display their real name, cost, types and text through the native card model;
Their illustrations are fetched from Scryfall into a private local cache and
displayed through the original card renderer, with artist credits. The first
launch needs an internet connection for these 31 images; cached art works offline.
Missing downloads use the native missing-art placeholder until a later launch.
No original Arena record is overwritten and no game assets are included in the
download. This enables the explicitly requested paper list, not all paper cards.

Tests distinguish cast/land entry points from named effect checks. The latter
cover Darien's damage tokens; life-gain chains; buffs; control and counters;
damage redirection; Worship; actual commander protection; five-Soldier exile;
conditional ramp; life-for-mana costs; blink, embalm and disturb; class levels;
upkeep/end-step damage; and token-dependent artifact abilities.
Testing one scenario is not proof of every possible interaction or game outcome.
