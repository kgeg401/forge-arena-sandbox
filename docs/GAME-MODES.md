# Choose a game mode

All modes below use Forge rules and the separately installed Arena presentation.
Private Commander connects two human players over the internet. Other extra
seats are bots on this PC. See [private multiplayer setup](MULTIPLAYER.md).

Open **Home > Play**, choose the listed tab and queue, then select a saved deck
and click **Play**.

| Mode | Tab and queue | Players and rules |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Practice | Play: Practice Bot | You and one Forge bot; 40–250-card deck. |
| Brawl | Brawl: Brawl Bot | 99 cards plus commander; 25 starting life. |
| Commander duel | Brawl: Commander Bot (40 Life) | 99 cards plus commander; 40 life, commander tax and 21 commander combat damage. |
| Private internet Commander | Brawl: Private Commander (Internet) | Two humans with their own saved decks; 40 life. Host and privately share an invitation; the other player joins. |
| Four-player Commander | Brawl: Commander Pod (4 Players) | You and three bots using complete Isamaru, Emry and Selvala decks. |
| Ten-player Commander | Brawl: Commander Pod (10 Players - Experimental) | You and nine bots; 40 life. Attack the nearest living opponent to your left while more than four players remain. At four or fewer, attack any legal opponent. |
| Zombie Horde with a commander | Brawl: Zombie Horde (Commander Deck) | Use your saved 99-card-plus-commander deck. You start at 40 life against a 200-card Zombie/Horror Horde. |
| Zombie Horde with a practice deck | Play: Zombie Horde (Solo) | Use a 40–250-card deck; 40 life against the same Horde. |

## Horde preparation and revival

Commander preparation equals the commander's original mana value, minimum one
turn: a five-mana commander gets five preparation turns and Darien gets six.
Practice decks get three turns. Normal land drops and mana costs still apply.
The Horde contains 120 physical Zombie token cards and 80 themed spells and
creatures, including 52 revival cards. Its badge shows cards remaining, not life.
See [Horde rules and deliberate variants](ZOMBIE-HORDE.md).

## Large-pod controls

The player rail shows each seat and current turn. Select a player to inspect
that board; return to automatic camera with **Tab**. At four surviving players,
the client returns to the smaller shared-table presentation. Public-zone
buttons inspect the corresponding graveyard or exile pile. Hidden cards stay
hidden. The ten-player format and its presentation remain experimental.

All deck construction still checks the enabled catalogue and commander color
identity. Experimental card admission is not proof of every interaction. Return
Home ends the match; closing the client does not create a durable match save.
