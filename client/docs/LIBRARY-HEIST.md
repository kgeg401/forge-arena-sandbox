# Grand Theft Library

Choose Grand Theft Library (Zaxara Wealth) from the client deck browser, then play a 40-life Commander match. It contains 99 main-deck cards and Zaxara, the Exemplary as commander. Every card is a real Magic card; paper-only cards retain their original rules and illustrations.

## Grand Theft Library (Zaxara Wealth)

Enchant an untap-ready Zaxara with Freed from the Real or Pemmin's Aura. Tap Zaxara for two blue mana, spend one to untap it, and repeat for as much mana as needed. Make green and black mana too, then cast Villainous Wealth with X at least the target opponent's remaining library size.

Wealth exiles those actual cards and offers eligible nonland spells to cast without paying their mana costs. These spells still need legal targets and resolve separately. Lands stay in exile. An opponent with an empty library loses when they next try to draw, unless another effect changes that result. This is not an automatic simultaneous cast of every card type.

Twincast with Twinning Staff creates two copies in addition to the original Wealth; choose legal targets for each. Put the Aura on Zaxara before Lightning Greaves, whose shroud prevents targeting. Finale of Devastation, Triumph of the Hordes and Torment of Hailfire supply alternate finishes.

## Real cards only

The invented Counterfeit the Multiverse card and its untouched supplied deck have been retired. They are absent from the playable catalogue, collection and starter decks, and custom-rule registration is disabled. User-edited decks retain every real card; if one contained the retired card, that slot needs a real replacement. Saved profiles are backed up before migration.

## Verified scope

Actual native-handler/Forge tests cover paid Zaxara untaps, Wealth exiling and casting, an empty-library draw loss, Staff/Twincast and supporting new cards. A separate 99-card seeded scaling fixture resolves 33 creature spells.

The prior two-deck demonstration also showed the now-retired invented variant; that segment no longer describes this client. Its real-card segment is an edited replay of seeded rules tests through the original renderer, with original game audio. It is not live input, a normally shuffled full match, a verified three-opponent win, or certification of every possible opposing library. The standalone replay holds the terminal board rather than displaying the normal match victory banner.

Beta 1 includes separately pinned Arena 14517 and 14589 overlays. The real-card win line passed through the installed 14517 native handlers; new native card, face, ability and localization rows were compared against both databases. This remains bounded interaction coverage; a second-household multiplayer test of this deck has not been performed.
